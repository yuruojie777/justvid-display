About iMog

An AI-powered face scan tool that turns a selfie into clear insights and a practical plan.

iMog helps you understand what’s already working in your face and what to improve next. Scan once, get your Attractiveness Score, then follow a short-term plan built around your results.

  • Fast front-face scan with camera or photo upload
  • Attractiveness Score with feature-level feedback
  • Personalized short-term improvement plan
  • Progress tracking over time

Photo Privacy
