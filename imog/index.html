FAQ

Short answers for now — easy to expand later.

What is iMog?

iMog is an AI face scan app that gives you an Attractiveness Score, a feature breakdown, and a personalized short-term improvement plan.

Is my photo data safe?

Photos are used to generate your analysis and plan. See the Privacy Policy for details. You can request deletion anytime.

Is this medical advice?

No. iMog is for informational self-improvement only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
