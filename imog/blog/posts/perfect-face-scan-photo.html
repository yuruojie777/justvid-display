How to Take the Perfect Face Scan Photo

Nov 26, 2026 · 6 min read
Perfect Face Scan

Your Attractiveness Score is only as good as the photo. If you want a fair AI rating, learn how to take the perfect face scan photo before you overthink the number.

Why the scan photo matters

Soft overhead light, chin-down selfies, and beauty filters hide structure. AI needs a clear front face to read symmetry, jawline, skin, and eye area cleanly.

Perfect face scan checklist

  • Angle: camera at eye level, face straight to lens — no tilt
  • Distance: full head and a little neck/shoulders in frame
  • Light: soft front light (window light works great)
  • Hair: off forehead, cheeks, and jawline
  • Expression: neutral, mouth closed, eyes open
  • Extras: no heavy makeup filters, no sunglasses, no harsh shadows
Tip: Take 10 extra seconds on the photo. One clean scan beats five rushed ones.

Best lighting setup (phone-friendly)

Stand facing a window. Turn off yellow overhead bulbs if they cast raccoon shadows. If you only have bathroom light, step back from the mirror light bar so it’s not blasting from above.

Common mistakes that tank your score

  • Chin tucked or head tilted for a “selfie angle”
  • Cropping the forehead or jaw out of frame
  • Heavy shadow on one side of the face
  • Smiling hard enough to change cheek and eye shape
  • Using a previous filtered social photo instead of a fresh scan

After you take the photo

Scan in iMog, read your Attractiveness Score and trait breakdown, then save that photo condition as your standard for future rescans. Same setup = honest progress.

FAQ

Should I smile in a face scan?

Neutral is better for structure scoring. Save the smile for socials.

Front camera or rear camera?

Rear cameras are usually sharper. Use a mirror or timer if you can keep eye-level framing.
