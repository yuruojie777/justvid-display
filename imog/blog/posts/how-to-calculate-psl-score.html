How to Calculate Your PSL Score

Nov 26, 2026 · 7 min read
Calculate PSL Score

“What’s my PSL?” is one of the most searched looksmaxxing questions. You don’t need a random Discord rater to calculate your PSL score — a structured face scan can estimate the number and the band in seconds.

Quick refresher: what PSL score means

A PSL score places your face on an attractiveness ladder used in looksmaxxing communities. Lower bands sit around low/mid Normie; higher bands move through Chadlite toward Chad and elite tiers. For the full map, read The Complete PSL Rating Guide.

How PSL scores are usually calculated

Serious ratings (human or AI) weigh several buckets, then map the result to a tier:

  • Harmony — overall balance between features
  • Structure / angularity — jaw, cheeks, brow, facial fat
  • Dimorphism — how masculine or feminine the face reads
  • Detail features — skin, eyes, brows, teeth, nose, contrast

No single feature “is” your PSL. A strong jaw with poor harmony still caps the score.

How to calculate your PSL score with AI

  1. Take a straight-on, well-lit front-face photo
  2. Run it through a face rating app that outputs a score + PSL band
  3. Review trait scores to see what pulled you up or down
  4. Save the result as your baseline
  5. Improve 2–3 levers, then rescan under the same conditions

In iMog, this shows up as your Attractiveness Score plus a PSL-style band — so you get the calculation and the language people actually use online.

Don’t average five rushed selfies. One clean scan is a better calculation than a noisy pile of takes.

Manual vs AI calculation

Manual PSL rating means posting a photo and collecting opinions. It’s subjective, biased, and hard to track. AI calculation is faster and more repeatable — especially when you keep lighting and angle consistent between rescans.

How to make the calculation fair

  • Same distance and camera height each time
  • Neutral expression, no smile stretch
  • No makeup filters if you want a true baseline
  • Compare scores from the same app only

FAQ

Can I calculate PSL from a side profile?

Side profiles help with projection and jaw assessment, but most PSL-style scores start from a clear front face. Use front for the headline score.

Why did two apps give different PSL scores?

Scales and weighting differ. Stick to one system (like iMog) when tracking progress.
