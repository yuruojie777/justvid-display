Looksmaxxing for Beginners

Looksmaxxing 101

New to the term and not sure what’s real vs internet theater? This is looksmaxxing for beginners — a practical starter guide focused on high-ROI habits, not extreme lore.

What is looksmaxxing?

Looksmaxxing means intentionally improving how you look — face, hair, skin, body, style — using a more systematic approach than “just gym and hope.” Online communities add rating systems (like PSL) and a lot of slang. You can use the useful parts and ignore the toxicity.

Softmaxxing vs hardmaxxing

  • Softmaxxing: skincare, haircut, brows, fat loss, posture, sleep, style, photo skill
  • Hardmaxxing: medical / surgical routes discussed online

Beginners should live in softmaxxing. It’s where almost everyone gets their first visible wins.

Beginner looksmaxxing plan (first 30 days)

  1. Scan your baseline — get an Attractiveness Score and trait read in iMog
  2. Fix the photo — learn the perfect face scan setup
  3. Pick 3 habits max — skin routine, sleep target, step/calorie consistency
  4. Grooming pass — haircut, brows, facial hair shape
  5. Rescan in 2–4 weeks — same lighting and angle
Beginner rule: if a tip needs a forum argument to justify, it’s not your week-one move.

High-ROI beginner priorities

  • Body fat in a healthier, leaner range
  • Skin clarity (simple routine > 12 products)
  • Haircut that fits head shape
  • Teeth / smile hygiene basics
  • Clothes that fit your shoulders and proportions

Rating systems without the spiral

PSL scores can help you track — they should not run your mood. Read What Is a Good PSL Score? and the complete PSL guide for context, then get back to habits.

FAQ

Is looksmaxxing only for men?

The internet culture skews male, but the core idea — structured appearance improvement — is universal.

How long until I see results?

Grooming and photo skill: days. Skin and fat loss: weeks. Rescan on a schedule so you’re not judging every mirror moment.
