How to Rate Your Face with AI

Nov 26, 2026 · 7 min read
AI Face Rating

Want an honest read on your looks without asking strangers online? AI face rating gives you a structured Attractiveness Score from one clear photo — plus the traits that pull the number up or down.

Why people rate their face with AI

Forum ratings are noisy. Friends are polite. Mirrors lie depending on lighting. AI face rating apps turn a front-face photo into a consistent score so you can track progress over time instead of guessing.

  • Fast baseline in under a minute
  • Breakdown by traits (jawline, skin, symmetry, and more)
  • A PSL-style band you can actually compare over rescans

How to rate your face with AI (step by step)

1. Open a face rating app

Choose an app that shows more than a single vanity number — look for trait scores, a PSL band, and a plan. That’s what iMog is built for.

2. Take a clean front-face scan

Straight to camera, soft front light, hair off the face, no filters. Bad photos create bad ratings — see How to Take the Perfect Face Scan Photo.

3. Read the score and the band

You’ll usually get a continuous Attractiveness Score (for example 7.4 / 10) and a ladder label like Mid-tier Normie or Chadlite. Read both: the number for precision, the band for context.

4. Check weak points, not just the headline

The useful part is the breakdown — skin, jawline, eye area, symmetry. Pick 2–3 levers you can improve in the next 7–30 days.

5. Follow a short plan and rescan

Rating without action is entertainment. Turn the report into habits, then rescan under the same conditions so the delta is real.

Tip: One clean scan + a short plan beats ten random ratings from different apps.

What a good AI face rating should include

  • Overall Attractiveness Score
  • PSL-style band (Normie → Chadlite → Chad…)
  • Trait-level scores you can act on
  • Guidance for what to improve next

Common mistakes

  • Using beauty filters or tilted selfies
  • Comparing scores across apps with different scales
  • Obsessing over 0.1 changes instead of clear weak points
  • Never rescanning after real habits stick

FAQ

Is AI face rating accurate?

It’s consistent enough to be useful as a baseline when photos are clean. It’s not a medical diagnosis and not a verdict on your life.

Can AI face rating help me looksmaxx?

Yes — if you use it to find weak points and track progress. Start with a scan in iMog, then follow the short plan built around your face.
