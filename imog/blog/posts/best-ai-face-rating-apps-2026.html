What to look for in an AI face rating app

  • Clean Attractiveness Score you can track over time
  • PSL band language (Normie / Chadlite / Chad…) if that’s how you research
  • Trait-level detail — jaw, skin, symmetry, eye area
  • Actionable plan — not just a roast
  • Rescan workflow so progress is visible

Other types of face rating tools you’ll see in 2026

Score-only web demos

Quick and free-feeling, but usually thin on breakdown and terrible for tracking. Fine for a one-off laugh; weak for improvement.

Community rating apps / forums

Human raters can be detailed — and also biased, rude, or inconsistent. Hard to use as a scientific baseline.

Filter-first “beauty camera” apps

Great for selfies, bad for truth. If the app’s job is to beautify the photo, it’s not a serious attractiveness rater.

Best stack in 2026: one serious AI rater (iMog) + consistent photos + a short habit plan.

How to choose quickly

  1. Do you get trait scores, or only one number?
  2. Can you rescan and compare?
  3. Does it tell you what to improve next?
  4. Are photos processed for analysis — not secretly filtered?
