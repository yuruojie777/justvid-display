What Is a Good PSL Score?

Nov 26, 2026 · 6 min read
Good PSL Score

What is a good PSL score? Online answers are loud and extreme. Here’s a calmer, useful map — so you know what average, strong, and rare actually look like.

The short answer

  • ~4 — average (mid-tier Normie territory)
  • ~5 — clearly above average / high-tier Normie to Chadlite
  • ~6+ — rare top-tier (Chad band and above)

A “good” score depends on your starting point. Moving 3.8 → 4.7 can change real-life first impressions more than fantasizing about a 7.

Good PSL score by goal

If you want to look solid in everyday life

Landing around high-4s to 5 is already a strong everyday face — especially with good style, skin, and body composition.

If you’re deep in looksmaxxing content

Forums treat 5+ as the interesting zone and 6 as elite. Remember those communities rate harsher than normal people.

If you’re tracking glow-up progress

The best PSL score is higher than last month under the same photo conditions. Trend > absolute flex.

Good = improved and maintainable. Not “perfect on a toxic forum scale.”

What actually raises a PSL score

  • Lower facial fat / better overall body fat
  • Skin clarity and under-eye quality
  • Haircut and brow shape that fit your bone structure
  • Posture and photo skill (underrated)
  • Sleep — soft faces often look tired, not “low genetic potential”

FAQ

Is 5 a good PSL score?

Yes — for most people, 5 is a genuinely strong result and well above average.

Is below 5 “over”?

No. That’s internet melodrama. Softmaxxing still moves a lot of faces in the 3–5 range.
