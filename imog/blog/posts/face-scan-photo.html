How to Take a Better Face Scan for Your Attractiveness Score

Nov 23, 2026 · 4 min read
Cover image placeholder

Your Attractiveness Score starts with one photo. A clearer front-face scan gives you a cleaner breakdown — and a plan that actually matches your face.

Why the photo matters

Soft lighting, odd angles, and heavy filters can hide structure. iMog needs a straight, well-lit front face so symmetry, jawline, skin, and other signals are easier to read.

Quick checklist

  • Face the camera straight on — no tilt, no side angle
  • Use soft front light (window light works well)
  • Keep hair off your forehead and jawline
  • Neutral expression, mouth closed
  • Skip beauty filters and heavy makeup if you want a true baseline

Common mistakes

Overhead bathroom lighting, chin-down selfies, and cropped foreheads are the usual score killers. If the scan feels “off,” retake with better light before you overthink the number.

Tip: One clean scan beats five rushed ones. Take 10 extra seconds on the photo.

What to do next

After your score, check the weakest pillar and follow the short daily plan. Rescan later under the same conditions so progress is easier to compare.
