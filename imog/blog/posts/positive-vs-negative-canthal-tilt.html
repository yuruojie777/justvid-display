Positive vs Negative Canthal Tilt

Nov 26, 2026 · 7 min read
Canthal Tilt

Canthal tilt shows up in almost every hunter-eyes and PSL discussion. Here’s a clear breakdown of positive vs negative canthal tilt — what it means, how to check yours, and how much it actually matters.

What is canthal tilt?

Canthal tilt is the angle of a line drawn from the inner corner of the eye (medial canthus) to the outer corner (lateral canthus).

  • Positive canthal tilt — outer corner sits higher than the inner corner
  • Neutral canthal tilt — roughly level
  • Negative canthal tilt — outer corner sits lower than the inner corner

Positive canthal tilt

A mild positive tilt is often linked with a sharper, more “hunter eye” look in looksmaxxing slang. It can make the eye area read more lifted and alert — especially with good brow support and low upper-eyelid exposure.

Negative canthal tilt

Negative tilt can make eyes look more downturned or tired in still photos. It’s common, and it does not automatically mean a low PSL score — harmony, skin, and overall structure still dominate.

How to check your canthal tilt

  1. Take a straight-on photo at eye level
  2. Zoom on one eye
  3. Compare inner corner height vs outer corner height
  4. Repeat on the other eye (asymmetry is normal)

In iMog, secondary traits can include canthal tilt as part of your eye-area read — useful as a label, not an obsession target.

Key point: canthal tilt is one trait. It rarely outweighs overall harmony and skin quality.

Can you change canthal tilt?

Softmaxxing won’t rewrite bone geometry. What you can improve around the eye area:

  • Sleep and allergy control (less puffiness)
  • Brow grooming / shaping
  • Body-fat reduction if the upper face looks soft
  • Photo angle and lighting (huge for first impressions)

Surgical options exist online as lore; they are not beginner looksmaxxing and not what you should chase first.

FAQ

Is positive canthal tilt required for hunter eyes?

It helps the look, but hunter eyes also involve brow bone, upper-lid exposure, and overall eye-area depth. See What Are Hunter Eyes?

Does negative canthal tilt ruin attractiveness?

No. Plenty of attractive faces have neutral or slightly negative tilt. Zoom out to the full face score before fixing one angle.
